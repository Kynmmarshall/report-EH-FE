SCENARIO

SecureTech Solutions Ltd. – Enterprise Security Assessment

SecureTech Solutions Ltd. is a growing software development and digital services company with
approximately 120 employees. The company provides web applications, cloud-based services, and remote
access for its staff. Recently, management observed several security incidents, including:

   •     Multiple unauthorized login attempts against internal systems.
   •     Employees using weak or reused passwords.
   •     Suspicious activities detected on the company web server.
   •     Sensitive customer information potentially exposed.
   •     Unpatched systems and outdated software.
   •     Lack of centralized monitoring and security documentation.

To strengthen its cybersecurity posture, the company has hired your cybersecurity consulting team to
perform a comprehensive Ethical Hacking and Security Assessment.

You have been granted written authorization to conduct all testing only within the provided laboratory
environment.

EXPECTED METHODOLOGY

Students are expected to follow a professional penetration testing methodology similar to PTES, NIST SP
800-115, or the OWASP Testing Guide.

A logical workflow should be demonstrated: Planning, Reconnaissance, Enumeration, Threat Modeling,
Vulnerability Assessment, Controlled Exploitation, Privilege Escalation, Post-Exploitation Analysis,
Traffic & Log Analysis, Risk Assessment, Security Hardening, Professional Reporting.
